About

I have been curious since I was a child, usually about too many things at once. That habit never went away, and it is why I enjoy looking at the same thing from several angles rather than settling into one. My degree is in tourism science, but the training kept widening along the way to include computer science, qualitative inquiry, and whatever else a question turned out to require.

I would rather chase what puzzles me than count what I publish. The projects I take on tend to be the ones I find genuinely curious, genuinely enjoyable, or genuinely difficult.

Research Interests

Information management, machine learning, blockchain, tourist behavior, and digital ethics.

Digital ethics is where most of my attention sits now. The National Research Foundation of Korea supports this line of work with a five-year grant of KRW 200 million, running from 2026 to 2031, on corporate digital responsibility in online travel platforms.

I have no preferred method. Qualitative, quantitative, or big data, I use whichever one the question actually calls for, and I am willing to pick up a new one when none of them fit.
